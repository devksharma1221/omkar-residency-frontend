Omkar Residency began in 2009 as the dream of the Kulkarni family — to create a place in Nashik where pilgrims, travellers and business guests could experience the gracious hospitality of a Maharashtrian home, with every modern comfort.

Over the years we have grown into a boutique hotel of 48 rooms and suites, a celebrated restaurant and a tranquil spa. Yet our essence remains unchanged: personal attention, honest food and spaces that invite you to slow down.
